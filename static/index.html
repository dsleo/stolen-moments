Each result is a candidate built from transposition-invariant pitch intervals. The ranking score helps order listening; it is not a probability of quotation or influence.

“Matched notes” counts the aligned phrase. Pitch agreement measures interval matches after limited edits. Rhythm agreement compares note spacing in beats. Distinctiveness reflects how widely the seed appears in this corpus. The score excerpts show stored pitches with approximate note values.

DTL1000 notes were automatically extracted and can contain transcription errors. When DTL1000 is involved, rhythm agreement compares note-onset timing after adjusting for tempo.

Direct video links use source-track times and may need timing correction for a particular upload. A quotation outside the indexed collections may be absent.
