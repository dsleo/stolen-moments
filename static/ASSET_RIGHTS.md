## Rights review required before advertising

- Album artwork is sourced from Apple/iTunes and Cover Art Archive. The map at `../data/album-art-map.json` contains 140 entries: 51 iTunes, 34 Cover Art Archive, and 55 not found. It records no reuse licences for those images. Do not include the covers in paid creative until permission is confirmed.
- Artist portrait links are external and mixed-source. Most map to Wikimedia Commons pages, but the map includes non-Commons sources as well. Check the specific file page, attribution, and commercial-use conditions for each selected portrait before reuse.
- Recording links point to third-party video uploads. Site links do not grant rights to download, edit, or include the recordings in a campaign. Use synthesized audio unless the exact master and composition rights have been cleared.
- The WJazzD-derived catalog is attributed and licensed under ODbL in `DATA_LICENSE.txt`. That data licence does not extend to album artwork, portraits, or sound recordings.

## Per-asset release record

Before campaign publication, list each selected asset, its original source URL, named rights holder or licence, required credit, allowed commercial uses, reviewer, and decision date. Keep the screenshot or clip composition restricted to cleared artwork. A blank cover placeholder or the project social card is the default when clearance is unknown.
