The passage catalog in `catalog/` is a **derived database** of the Weimar Jazz
Database (WJazzD) v2.1, produced by the Jazzomat Research Project (HfM Weimar).
WJazzD is published under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/);
its individual contents are under the
[Database Contents License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/).

**Attribution.** Contains information from WJazzD v2.1 (Pfleiderer, Frieler,
Abeßer, Zaddach & Burkhart, eds., *Inside the Jazzomat: New Perspectives for
Jazz Research*, 2017), available at
<https://jazzomat.hfm-weimar.de/download/download.html>.

**Changes.** Solo transcriptions were re-indexed and aligned in pairs to find
shared phrases. The catalog stores each matched passage with its note positions,
alignment, similarity score, solo metadata, and recording links. Scores are
heuristics and are not part of the source data.

**Share-alike.** This derived catalog is made available under the ODbL 1.0. If
you redistribute it or a further adapted database, keep this notice and the same

**Not covered by this licence.**

- Recording links point to third-party YouTube videos. JazzTube alignments are
  from <http://mir.audiolabs.uni-erlangen.de/jazztube/>.
- Artist portraits keep their own sources and terms.
